Beyond the Finish Line: Exploring Running as a Hobby in Australia

From everyday joggers to marathon runners, uncovering participation trends, competitive running and the benefits of staying active.

01 - INTRODUCTION

More than just putting one foot in front of the other

From a casual jog around the neighbourhood to training consistently
for a marathon, running offers something for people at every
level. For many runners, there is a reason to run,
whether it is staying healthy, relieving stress,
building friendships, or achieving personal
goals. Let's take a deeper dive into the
trends behind this popular hobby.
